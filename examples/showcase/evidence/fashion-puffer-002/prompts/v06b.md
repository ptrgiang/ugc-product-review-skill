# FASHION-PUFFER-002 — V06B prompt

Status: **ready for generation — production-optimized try-on**

## Experiment objective

Generate the most believable usable UGC try-on clip without requiring a fragile literal asymmetric opening state.

Priority:

```text
natural motion
> readable sleeve interaction
> final fit proof
> strict partial-state opening
```

Unlike V06A, this branch does not require a supplied literal first-frame image.

## Generation prompt

```text
Create one realistic 8-second vertical 9:16 smartphone UGC fashion try-on video in an ordinary bright bedroom.

Use the same creator and the exact same navy quilted jacket throughout the entire video.

Preserve the creator's face, hair, complexion, body proportions, white fitted long-sleeve top, black wide-leg pants, and white shoes.

Preserve the jacket's exact navy color, padded quilting, body length, collar shape, centered zipper, sleeve proportions, cuffs, material, hem, scale, and overall silhouette.

The clip should feel like a real casual social-media try-on, not a fashion commercial.

0:00–0:01.5 — FAST NATURAL SETUP
Start with the creator already positioning the jacket around her torso.

The right side is immediately close to the right shoulder and the right arm begins entering or is nearly seated in the right sleeve.

Do not spend time presenting the jacket, holding it up to camera, unfolding it, or picking it up from elsewhere.

The viewer should understand within the first second that she is putting the jacket on.

0:01.5–0:04.8 — CLEAR LEFT-SLEEVE ACTION
Let the right side become naturally seated first.

Then keep the LEFT-arm insertion clearly visible.

The creator briefly looks toward the empty left sleeve opening, guides the left hand into it, and pushes the left arm through in one continuous motion.

Show clear hand-to-sleeve contact.
Show believable padded-fabric resistance and compression.
Keep the sleeve action visible in front or beside the torso rather than hiding it behind the body.

Do not let the jacket instantly snap into a fully worn state.

0:04.8–0:06.5 — SETTLE
The jacket naturally settles onto both shoulders.

The creator makes one small adjustment near the collar or front panel.

The padded fabric reacts with believable weight, folds, and compression.

0:06.5–0:08.0 — FIT PROOF
Hold a clean full-body view long enough to read the final silhouette.

The creator faces the camera, lightly touches the front of the jacket, and gives a restrained satisfied expression.

Keep the final fully worn state visible for at least one second.

CAMERA
Vertical 9:16 smartphone video.
Static tripod-like phone or extremely subtle handheld movement.
Natural bedroom daylight.
Minor realistic autofocus or exposure breathing is acceptable.

No cinematic orbit.
No dramatic push-in.
No dolly move.
No fashion-film choreography.

PHYSICS
Hands visibly contact the garment before moving it.
Sleeves maintain constant attachment and length.
The jacket has believable padded weight.
Shoulders, elbows, wrists, and hands follow normal human movement.
The jacket may wrinkle and compress naturally but must not change design.

EDITING
Prefer one continuous shot.

If a cut is absolutely necessary, use only one simple hard cut and preserve body pose, creator identity, garment geometry, and dressing state across it.

No transitions.
No speed ramp.
No montage.

NEGATIVE CONSTRAINTS
- no garment morphing
- no instant fully-worn teleport
- no disappearing or duplicated sleeve
- no detached jacket panel
- no extra buttons, pockets, logos, trims, or design changes
- no hand deformation
- no extra fingers
- no arm passing through fabric
- no creator disappearance
- no constant smiling
- no staged commercial posing
- no captions
- no logo card
```

## Pass condition

V06B is production-usable when:

```text
1. product and try-on intent are readable within the first second
2. right side settles quickly without major artifact
3. left-arm insertion remains visible and physically continuous
4. no major garment morph or body-through-fabric event occurs
5. product geometry remains recognizable throughout
6. final fit is held clearly for at least one second
7. creator behavior feels casual rather than choreographed
```

An initial setup state is allowed. Do not fail V06B merely because frame 1 is not the asymmetric partial-worn state required by V06A.
