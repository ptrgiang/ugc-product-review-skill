# FASHION-PUFFER-002 — V01 QA

Status: **failed — P1 Major**

## Evidence reviewed

V01 Clip 1 attempted a full puffer-jacket dressing interaction. V01 Clip 2 provided an already-worn fit-proof continuation.

## Verdict

Clip 1 fails during the full dressing motion. The garment partially morphs/snaps onto the creator instead of following a physically continuous arm-to-sleeve and shoulder-loading sequence.

Clip 2 is substantially more stable and should be preserved by default unless an independent defect appears.

## Root cause

The shot asks the model to solve too many dependent constraints at once:

```text
both arms
+ both sleeves
+ shoulder rotation
+ flexible quilted fabric
+ changing occlusion
+ garment geometry continuity
```

The dominant issue is therefore interaction complexity, not general product identity.

## What worked

- jacket color and broad silhouette remain recognizable;
- the already-worn continuation is more stable than the dressing motion;
- the fit-proof concept itself remains useful;
- the failure is localized enough to justify targeted repair rather than rewriting the whole case.

## Repair direction

V02 should avoid a full two-arm dressing sequence. Preserve material proof and the already-worn fit shot, but reduce the fragile transition to a partial-state one-arm insertion:

```text
held
→ hard cut
→ right arm already inserted
→ left arm enters left sleeve
→ garment settles
```
