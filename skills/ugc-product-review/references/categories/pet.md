# Category: Pet Products

## Priorities
- safe interaction
- natural animal behavior
- correct product scale
- owner context
- setup/cleanup

## Rules
Do not force animals into unnatural poses.

Avoid complex choreography.

## Strong archetypes
- First Reaction
- Routine
- Problem → Solution
- Setup Demo
- Honest Review

## Hero moments
- pet approaches/uses product naturally
- easy setup
- cleanup
- comfort/interest

## Negative constraints
- no forced pose
- no impossible pet movement
- no changing animal identity
- no unsafe interaction
