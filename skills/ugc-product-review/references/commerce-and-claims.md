# Commerce and Claims Module

Load for affiliate, paid UGC, factual claims, regulated categories, or testimonial-like content.

## Commerce modes

### Organic
- genuine personal tone
- usually no CTA
- minimal selling

### Affiliate
- review first
- proof before recommendation
- soft CTA only at end when requested

### Paid UGC
- clearer problem/benefit
- CTA allowed
- retain creator realism

## Claim types

### Observable
Can be shown directly.

### Subjective
Personal reaction.

### Factual
Requires reliable support.

### Regulated/high-risk
Requires additional caution.

Prefer observable and subjective claims.

Do not invent:
- percentages
- battery duration
- time savings
- technical specs
- medical outcomes
- guaranteed results

## Health / wellness / cosmetics

Prefer:
- texture
- packaging
- application
- routine
- subjective feel
- visible cosmetic appearance

Avoid unsupported treatment or disease claims.

## Testimonial authenticity

Do not fabricate:
- verified purchase
- long-term usage history
- expert credentials
- clinical testing
- real customer review

If creating fictional UGC direction, treat it as scripted creative direction, not factual evidence.

## Long-use review

If user asks for "after 30 days" without real observations:
- do not invent experience
- create a script framework that requires real observations before publishing

## Disclosure

For published commercial content, user should ensure required sponsorship/affiliate disclosures and local advertising compliance.
