# Brand / Advertising / Marketing slash presets

Use these presets when the user explicitly wants commercial creative rather than default organic-looking UGC.

- `/adcreative` — Advertising key visual with a strong focal point and usable space for copy.
- `/commercial` — Commercial treatment with clear product/message hierarchy.
- `/campaignvisual` — Primary campaign visual with a coherent campaign idea.
- `/brandposter` — Brand-led poster with professional advertising composition.
- `/socialad` — Paid-social creative for Facebook, Instagram, TikTok, or similar placements.
- `/promobanner` — Promotion/offer banner with readable hierarchy.
- `/launchposter` — Product/service launch visual emphasizing newness and reveal.
- `/offercreative` — Offer-led creative emphasizing promotion, CTA, and supported benefits.
- `/billboardstyle` — Simple, bold, high-readability composition inspired by outdoor advertising.
- `/premiumad` — High-end advertising treatment with refined light, material, and space.

## UGC integration notes

These presets intentionally shift the output away from the skill's default organic UGC look. Use them only when explicitly invoked.

Commercial presets do not authorize invented claims, fake testimonials, unsupported savings, fabricated reviews, or misleading before/after results. Load `../commerce-and-claims.md` when claim-sensitive copy or paid/affiliate content is involved.
