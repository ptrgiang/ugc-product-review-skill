# Fashion / Model slash presets

Use these presets for wearable-product presentation. Preserve garment geometry, fit-relevant construction, color, material, and creator identity when references exist.

- `/modellook` — Put the fashion product on a model in a commercially readable way.
- `/lookbook` — Create a polished fashion lookbook/editorial composition with a coherent concept.
- `/streetstyle` — Natural modern outdoor/street styling suitable for social media.
- `/runway` — Catwalk/runway-inspired presentation with a clear silhouette and fashion focus.
- `/fashioneditorial` — Strong magazine-style fashion image with deliberate pose, lighting, and concept.
- `/catalogmodel` — Straightforward catalog-style model image where the garment/product is easy to inspect.
- `/outfitfocus` — Keep attention on the outfit and reduce distracting background detail.
- `/luxuryfashion` — Premium fashion treatment with restrained high-end styling.
- `/minimalfashion` — Clean, modern, sparse composition with minimal props.
- `/seasoncampaign` — Seasonal campaign treatment such as summer, autumn, winter, Lunar New Year, or another user-specified season.

## UGC integration notes

For review-video workflows, prefer `/catalogmodel`, `/outfitfocus`, `/streetstyle`, or `/modellook` when the goal is fit proof rather than editorial spectacle.

Do not let `/runway`, `/fashioneditorial`, or `/luxuryfashion` silently make a UGC request look like a polished commercial. Use those treatments only when explicitly invoked.

When a garment must be worn or removed, keep body mechanics, sleeve paths, fabric resistance, gravity, and state continuity plausible.
