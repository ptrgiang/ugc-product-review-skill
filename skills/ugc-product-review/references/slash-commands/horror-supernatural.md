# Horror / Supernatural slash presets

Use these presets only when explicitly requested. They are stylistic modifiers and should not silently affect ordinary product-review work.

- `/horror` — Dark, tense horror atmosphere with depth and controlled visual unease.
- `/horrorposter` — Strong horror-story or horror-film poster composition.
- `/ghostscene` — Supernatural scene involving a ghostly or unexplained presence.
- `/darkcinematic` — Dark cinematic treatment with cool tones, contrast, mystery, and restrained visibility.
- `/folkhorror` — Folk-horror treatment grounded in rural settings, belief systems, legends, or traditional atmosphere.
- `/hauntedhouse` — Haunted/abandoned-house setting with unsettling environmental cues.
- `/ritualscene` — Cinematic ritual/occult-style scene with symbolic staging.
- `/creepythumbnail` — Horror thumbnail with a clear subject and legible unsettling mood.
- `/moodhorror` — Mood-first horror frame emphasizing light, mist, shadow, and unease.
- `/supernatural` — Mysterious supernatural treatment with controlled unreal elements.

## UGC integration notes

If a horror preset is combined with a product-review request, keep the actual product recognizable and physically consistent. Treat the horror layer as campaign styling, not permission to distort the product or fabricate product behavior.

Do not introduce unrelated supernatural claims about a real product unless the user is clearly creating fictional/entertainment content.
