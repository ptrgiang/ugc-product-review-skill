# Portrait / People / Character slash presets

Use these presets for creator references, character consistency, and human-focused supporting assets.

- `/portrait` — Clear balanced portrait with readable face and natural lighting.
- `/headshot` — Professional headshot for profile/corporate-style presentation.
- `/cinematicportrait` — Portrait with more dramatic cinematic light and emotional depth.
- `/reallifeshot` — Natural everyday photographic treatment that avoids over-staging.
- `/beautyshot` — Clean beauty-focused portrait with soft light and facial emphasis.
- `/characterdesign` — Define a repeatable character appearance, wardrobe, and visual identity.
- `/characterref` — Create a reference sheet/asset for repeated use across scenes.
- `/expressionboard` — Same character shown with multiple distinct expressions in one reference layout.
- `/costumetest` — Compare multiple wardrobe or styling variants for the same character.
- `/identitylock` — Prioritize stable face, hair, complexion, age appearance, and recognizable identity across outputs.

## UGC integration notes

For UGC production, `/characterref` and `/identitylock` are the most useful continuity presets. Use them to strengthen creator consistency without making the creator look synthetic or over-retouched.

`/reallifeshot` should reinforce the skill's default preference for ordinary environments, natural light, casual posture, and restrained expression.

Do not infer or invent personal identity details that are not supplied or visibly supported. A reference defines visual continuity, not biography or credentials.
