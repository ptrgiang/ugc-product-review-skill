# Product / E-commerce slash presets

Use these presets for product-focused visual assets. Preserve the exact product reference whenever one is supplied.

- `/productshot` — Studio-style advertising product image: clean, sharp, product-first composition.
- `/packshot` — Catalog/advertising packshot with a controlled simple background and clear product geometry.
- `/ecommerce` — Marketplace-ready sales image for e-commerce listings; prioritize clarity, product readability, and conversion utility.
- `/whitebg` — Place the product on a clean white background suitable for marketplace/catalog use.
- `/transparentcutout` — Isolate the product/subject for a transparent-background cutout; preserve edges and geometry.
- `/lifestyleproduct` — Place the product in a believable real-life usage context with commercial usefulness but natural behavior.
- `/premiumproduct` — Elevate the product with refined lighting, materials, composition, and restrained premium styling.
- `/macrodetail` — Close-up of material, texture, stitching, controls, surface finish, or another small product detail.
- `/flatlay` — Top-down composition with the product and supporting props arranged cleanly and intentionally.
- `/productcomparison` — Show 2–3 products/options in one readable comparative composition without inventing unsupported differences.

## UGC integration notes

For UGC workflows:

- `/productshot` and `/packshot` are useful as reference/hero stills, not substitutes for authentic creator footage.
- `/lifestyleproduct` should stay observational and physically plausible when feeding a later video prompt.
- `/macrodetail` can define a proof shot or insert shot.
- `/productcomparison` must use only known differences; never fabricate specs, price, performance, or claims.

If the user supplies a product reference, do not redesign logos, controls, openings, accessories, proportions, materials, or colorways unless explicitly requested.
