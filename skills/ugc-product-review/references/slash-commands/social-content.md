# YouTube / TikTok / Social slash presets

Use these presets for social-media support assets and attention-oriented frames.

- `/thumbnail` — Clear high-readability YouTube thumbnail designed to remain legible at small size.
- `/viralthumbnail` — Strong contrast, clear subject, and curiosity while staying faithful to the content.
- `/youtubecover` — Main YouTube visual/cover treatment.
- `/tiktokcover` — Vertical TikTok/Reels cover with clear subject and title-safe composition.
- `/socialpost` — Clean social-post layout with readable hierarchy.
- `/carouselpost` — Consistent multi-card/carousel visual system.
- `/quotegraphic` — Quote/storytelling graphic centered on a short text message.
- `/channelart` — YouTube/Facebook channel banner with safe areas for key content.
- `/episodetitlecard` — Episode/series title frame with repeatable visual identity.
- `/hookframe` — Opening frame optimized to stop scrolling in the first seconds of a video.

## UGC integration notes

`/hookframe` is especially useful inside the normal creative-strategy route. It should translate the chosen hook into one visually readable opening frame without adding unrelated spectacle.

For `/thumbnail` or `/viralthumbnail`, curiosity must remain truthful to the actual product/result. Do not depict a result, feature, emotion, or comparison that the content does not support.

For cover/title assets, generated text may be unreliable; when exact copy matters, define text-safe space and recommend adding final typography in post rather than depending on image/video generation to render exact wording.
