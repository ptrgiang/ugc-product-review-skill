# Storyboard / Video / Cinema slash presets

Use these presets for planning, framing, continuity, or supporting pre-production assets.

- `/storyboard` — Convert a concept/script into sequential storyboard frames.
- `/shotlist` — Turn an idea into a practical ordered list of camera shots.
- `/cinematicframe` — One deliberately composed frame with cinematic light and visual language.
- `/filmscene` — A complete scene description with subject, environment, action, and emotion.
- `/establishingshot` — Wide opening shot that clearly establishes place and spatial context.
- `/mediumshot` — Medium framing suited to dialogue, product handling, and body-language readability.
- `/closeupshot` — Close-up emphasizing face, product detail, texture, or a key reaction.
- `/actionframe` — Motion-rich frame for a peak action or dynamic beat.
- `/behindthescenes` — Behind-the-scenes visual treatment with informal production context.
- `/continuityframe` — Reference frame emphasizing continuity of creator, wardrobe, product, props, and environment across shots.

## UGC integration notes

`/storyboard`, `/shotlist`, and `/continuityframe` map directly to UGC production planning.

Do not let `/cinematicframe` or `/filmscene` introduce unnecessary camera choreography into a realistic UGC request. Product-review authenticity still has priority unless the user explicitly asks for a cinematic treatment.

For `/storyboard`, every frame should have a purpose such as hook, action, proof, reaction, or verdict. Avoid decorative frames that do not advance the review.

For `/continuityframe`, explicitly preserve the product state, creator state, camera side, environment, and hand/object positions that matter for the next shot.
